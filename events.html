Before you come

How the nights run

Where is the entrance?

Broad St, behind Hai Di Bao. Look for the plain black door with no sign. The door staff will find you before you find them.

Do I need to be on the guest list?

Not always, but it is faster and cheaper. Guest list closes at 23:00 on club nights, and we hold the queue for it.

Can I book a table?

Yes, you can book the VIP booth upstairs by contacting one of our members of management.
