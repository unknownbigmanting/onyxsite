Answers

Common questions

Do I need to book to get in?

No. Walk-ins are welcome until the room is full — guest list just gets you in faster and cheaper. On specials, book ahead.

Where exactly is the entrance?

Vane Street, under the Ninth Street overpass, three steps down from street level. Black steel door, no sign. Ring the buzzer.

Can I hire the club for a private event?

Yes, on Mondays. The Neon Room seats 40 and the full venue takes 85. Message us with the date and we will come back with a price.

How do I get a table?

Send the form with the date and your group size. Tables are confirmed by reply, and held until 23:30.
